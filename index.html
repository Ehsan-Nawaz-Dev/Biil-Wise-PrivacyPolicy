Introduction & Scope

Welcome to BillWise Pakistan ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy details our policies and procedures regarding the processing, storage, and protection of information when you use our mobile application.

BillWise Pakistan is designed specifically for residents and businesses across Pakistan to organize, track, and receive timely local reminders for their recurring utility bills (Electricity, Gas, Water, and Internet services).

Key Principle: Privacy by Default

Unlike traditional apps that force you to register on an external server and upload your utility bills to cloud databases, BillWise Pakistan operates offline-first. All consumer reference numbers, bill due dates, and payment history records remain exclusively inside your device's private sandbox storage.

  • We do not track your GPS geographic coordinates or real-time location.
03

Offline-First Architecture

BillWise Pakistan is architected on an offline-first foundation. All data persistence is performed locally on your physical device using encrypted application sandbox storage (`AsyncStorage`).

This ensures that:

  • Zero Cloud Syncing: Your utility bills and reference numbers are not transmitted to any remote company server, database, or analytics bucket owned by us.
  • Offline Functionality: You can open, view, track, search, and update your utility accounts and payment histories without any active Internet connection.
  • Device Encapsulation: Other applications on your smartphone cannot access BillWise Pakistan's internal sandbox files, respecting Android's strict application isolation model.

07

Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because all your data is stored locally on your device and never uploaded to our servers, you have absolute control over retention and deletion:

  • Deleting an Account / Bill in App: You can remove any individual utility account or bill record at any time via the in-app deletion button.
  • Complete Wipe via App Settings: You can clear all cached data by navigating to Android Settings > Apps > BillWise Pakistan > Storage > Clear Storage / Data.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling BillWise Pakistan immediately and permanently purges all local databases, accounts, reminders, and cached files from your phone.

No residual backup copies remain on any cloud server because no remote synchronization exists.

By maintaining all user records within local offline sandbox storage, user confidentiality and statutory compliance are preserved by design.
