Never miss a utility bill due date again! BillWise Pakistan is your premier, offline-first utility bill organizer and reminder app designed to help households, shopkeepers, and property managers effortlessly organize, track, and monitor electricity, gas, water, and internet bills across Pakistan.

Tired of paying late payment surcharges on your LESCO, K-Electric, or SNGPL bills? With BillWise Pakistan, save all your consumer reference numbers in one secure place, track monthly dues, and receive timely local push reminders before every due date—even without an active internet connection!

🔔 Smart Due Date Reminders

📊 Expense History & Analytics
